About Me Page

Hi, my name is Zechariah (Zac) Diegelman. I’m 24 years old, married, and a proud owner of a Mini Australian Shepherd puppy.

My journey to college has been unique, I took five years after high school to explore different career paths and discover my true calling. During that time, I worked in a variety of roles: from building cabinets in a manufacturing shop, to leading landscaping crews, to renovating bathrooms, to caring for over 200 goats on a farm in Pennsylvania. In every job, I stepped up as a leader, worked hard, and gave my best effort. These experiences taught me adaptability, problem-solving, and the importance of persistence.

What drives me most is growth, personal, professional, educational, and spiritual. That mindset led me back to school, where I’m now pursuing my goals with focus and determination. My faith is central to who I am, and it shapes the way I approach both people and challenges, with kindness, dedication, and the desire to make a positive impact.

I also grew up as a missionary kid, living in Costa Rica and Honduras, where I became fluent in Spanish. Those experiences gave me a global perspective and a heart for people of all backgrounds.

Ultimately, I’m motivated by the challenge of learning, building, and contributing to something bigger than myself. Whether in the classroom, in the workplace, or in my community, I strive to grow and give my best every day.
